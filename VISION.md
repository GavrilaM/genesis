# Vision

Simulate causes, not stories. Canonical state and measured transitions precede
interpretation. Species are configurable biological populations, without cultural
destinies. Aggregate cohorts have no invented personal histories.

Only Prototype 001 is authorized here: environment, renewable food, populations,
heritable variation, migration and evidence. Individuals, society, technology,
religion, magic, graphics and LLM runtime calls remain outside this milestone.
Full future context: `docs/GENESIS_WORLD_VISION.md`.
